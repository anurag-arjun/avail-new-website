USP

Verifiable by Design

Avail DA guarantees data integrity through a combination of validity proofs, KZG polynomial commitments, and data availability sampling (DAS). Every block posted to Avail can be independently verified by light clients, even on resource-constrained devices, without needing to download the entire dataset. This means applications built on Avail inherit cryptographic proof of data availability, enabling trustless interoperability and secure rollup settlement across L2s and L3s.

use cases

Avail DA powers it all

Avail DA powers every kind of chain, from high-performance rollups to privacy-preserving apps, giving you verifiable data availability at any scale.

Scale Rollups Efficiently

Scale Validiums, Optimiums, or Sovereign Rollups without compromising verifiability. Avail DA combines KZG commitments, DAS, and a ZK bridge that lets rollups and light clients prove data inclusion and settle securely on-chain.

Power Based and Shared Sequencer Chains

Use Avail DA as the foundation for based or shared sequencing. Gain trust-minimized visibility and interoperability across rollups and domains, backed by verifiable proofs.

Deliver Instant Experiences

Give users lightning-fast responsiveness with 250ms preconfirmations and 20s finality. Ideal for gaming, social, and high-frequency apps where speed and reliability define the experience.

Protect Sensitive and Private Data

Keep data private while staying verifiable with encrypted DA. Confidential state updates remain provably available, enabling secure, privacy-preserving apps across DeFi, RWAs, and institutional use cases.
